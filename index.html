Mengsha Liu

I am an LLM algorithm engineer at ByteDance in Singapore, working on agentic search: models that decompose and rewrite their own queries and orchestrate search tools over long, multi-turn trajectories.

I work on both halves of that loop — agentic RL with verifiable and rubric-based rewards for long-horizon tool use, and the harness around it: action-space design, context management, and the data engine that synthesises trajectories and mines rollouts. Evaluation should score the process, not only the answer.

Previously: core search at Huawei, open-world game agents at Tencent AI Lab, multimodal document and chart understanding at Alibaba DAMO Academy. M.Sc. from NTU, B.Eng. from Sun Yat-sen University with Prof. Ying Shen. Open to collaboration.
